This is every line of the Hindi website, page by page, with the English beside it. The Hindi pages are not live yet: they go live only after you have read them and are happy with them.

**How to correct:** if a Hindi line is wrong, or does not sound like you, write your version next to it (or tell Arpit which line, and how you would say it). Lines that are fine need nothing.

A few things to know:

- Words in **bold** in a heading are shown in colour, with a line under them.
- Some English words are kept as people say them: consultation, WhatsApp, Ph.D. The business name stays in English.
- Lines that are still missing in English too (for example, what each consultation covers, class timings, the next batch) are not shown here. They will be translated when they are written.
- Clients’ testimonials are shown in the language they wrote them in, and are not translated.
